# After Effects Presets: Anima Ez

A collection of professional presets for Adobe After Effects, designed to accelerate your creative workflow. Crafted with precision to deliver high-quality results in seconds.

## 🚀 Key Features
*   **High Efficiency:** Save time on complex animation tasks.
*   **Customizable:** All presets are fully adjustable, including Keyframes, Expressions, and Effect Controls.
*   **Clean Workflow:** Designed not to disrupt your project structure.
*   **Compatibility:** Compatible with After Effects (CC 2020 and later).

## 📦 Collection Content
This project includes 7 presets divided into the following categories:
1.  **Digital Look**
2.  **Glitch FX**
3.  **Hand Drawn**
4.  **RGB Displacement**
5.  **Rim Light**
6.  **Texture**
7.  **Trim Paths**

## 🛠 Installation Guide
1.  Download the `.ffx` files from this repository.
2.  Locate the **Presets** folder in your Adobe After Effects directory:
    *   **Windows:** `C:\Users\[Username]\Documents\Adobe\After Effects [Version]\User Presets`
    *   **macOS:** `/Users/[Username]/Documents/Adobe/After Effects [Version]/User Presets`
3.  Copy the `.ffx` files into that folder.
4.  Restart After Effects.
5.  Find the presets in the **Effects & Presets** panel (Shortcut: `Ctrl+5` / `Cmd+5`).

## 💡 How to Use
1.  Select a layer (Shape, Text, or Solid) in your timeline.
2.  Open the **Effects & Presets** panel.
3.  Search for the preset name in the search bar.
4.  Double-click or drag and drop the preset onto your selected layer.
5.  Adjust the Keyframes or Effect Controls to suit your visual needs.
